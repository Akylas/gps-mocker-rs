GPS Mocker feeds a fake position to your device — a single point you drop on the
map, or a whole route replayed at a speed that behaves like a real drive.

It is a developer tool. Testing anything that reacts to where you are — a
navigation app, a geofence, a ride tracker — otherwise means going outside.

SETTING IT UP

Android does not let an app mock a location until you say so. Open Settings →
Developer options → "Select mock location app" and pick GPS Mocker. The app says
whether it has been selected, and offers a shortcut straight to that screen.
No root, and no other app is touched.

WHAT IT DOES

* Replays a route with a speed model that eases into corners, follows the speed
  a GPX was recorded at when it carries timestamps, and can loop.
* Imports GPX and GeoJSON. A GeoJSON export from a routing app brings its
  turn-by-turn instructions along; a GPX can be map-matched to get some.
* Builds a route from waypoints through a Valhalla routing server, with the full
  set of costing options — highways, tolls, ferries, top speed, and the rest.
* Drives manually with an on-screen pad, and routes you back to the line when
  you wander off it.
* Saves routes to a local library, and exports them again as GPX.
* Map with terrain, hillshade and 3D. Light and dark, following the system.
  English and French.

Playback runs from a foreground service, so a route keeps going after you leave
the app. When that notification appears is up to you: while a route is playing,
for the whole session, or never.

THE DESKTOP APP

The same project builds a desktop app for macOS, Linux and Windows that drives
an *attached* device instead — over adb for an Android phone or emulator, and
over the Simulator or a lockdown service for iOS. It installs and configures
this app for you, so the phone under test shows nothing on screen at all.

Free software, MIT licensed. Source, releases and issues:
https://github.com/Akylas/gps-mocker-rs

NETWORK

Route building, map matching, place search and the basemap are fetched from
online services — a Valhalla instance and a MapTiler style by default. Both are
configurable in Settings, so you can point the app at your own. Nothing else
leaves the device: there is no account, no analytics and no tracking.
